## By role

- **Developer or workflow designer:** begin with the promise, authoritative
  state, attempts, checkpoints, and terminal outcomes.
- **Architect:** add ownership transfers, compensation, decomposition,
  migration, and evidence joins.
- **Operations:** add stuck-state detection, deadlines, escalation, recovery,
  and reconciliation.
- **Product or service owner:** name the beneficiary, acceptable endings,
  residual harm, and authority to close.
- **AI builder:** ensure the model session is not the durable owner or state
  store and that every consequential tool path remains governed.
