# Start Here: One Promise in Thirty Minutes

**Reader route:** Complete the thirty-minute exercise output first. The
facilitator-only freeze, manifest, and evidence protocol is a separate route
for controlled pilots; do not open it until the first-pass artifact is done.

Choose one process that can wait, cross a service or human boundary, produce a
partial effect, or outlive the request that started it. Do not begin with the
workflow diagram.

## 0–5 minutes: name the person and outcome

Complete three sentences:

1. We owe ___ this outcome: ___;
2. today they wait or chase because ___;
3. dependable coordination would unlock ___.

If no beneficiary or owed outcome can be named, you may be automating activity
rather than a business promise.

## 5–15 minutes: define responsibility

Complete the [seven-line first pass](workflow-responsibility-and-progress-brief.md#ten-minute-first-pass)
in the Responsibility-and-Progress Brief. Compare it with the
[miniature Northbridge example](workflow-responsibility-and-progress-brief.md#miniature-example).

Stop if ownership disappears while waiting or if “success” names only a step.

## 15–25 minutes: interrupt the happy path

Choose one interruption: lost response after a possible effect, human delay,
deadline exhaustion, or a version change while work is open. In the
[Compensation-and-Failure Matrix](compensation-and-failure-matrix.md), record who
may retry, reconcile, compensate, escalate, accept residue, or stop.

## 25–30 minutes: ask for a read-back

Give the seven lines to someone outside the process team. Ask what remains owed,
who owns the next move, how the promise may legitimately end, and what proves
closure.

## Your first result

You now have a reviewable workflow hypothesis, not a validated process. Continue
with the [Northbridge example](examples/northbridge-workflow-responsibility-and-progress-brief.md),
the [Aster Vale transfer example](examples/aster-vale-workflow-responsibility-and-progress-brief.md),
and the time, failure, approval, and migration tools required by the promise.
