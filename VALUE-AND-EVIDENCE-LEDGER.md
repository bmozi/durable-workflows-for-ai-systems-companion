# Value and Evidence Ledger

Use this to connect workflow architecture to an outcome without turning a
proposal into an invented business case.

## Five-minute first row

| Open promise | Who benefits | Current friction or exposure | Architectural change | Evidence to collect | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Partner credit after a disputed invoice | Partner and service team | Timeouts conceal whether the credit was committed; manual reconciliation | Durable state, idempotent effect, deadline, reconciliation | Duplicate-effect count, open-promise age, closure receipts, manual touches | Pilot, constrain, redesign, or expand |

## Evidence rules

- Label a number **observed**, **reported**, **estimated**, or **unknown**.
- Do not substitute job success, queue depth, or model confidence for business
  completion.
- Count manual reconciliation, compensation, migration, and incident work.
- Record negative evidence and unresolved obligations.
- Do not claim savings until the measurement window, denominator, and source
  are named.

## Monday-morning use

Choose one process people already chase manually. Establish its current open-
promise age and manual-touch baseline before changing the architecture. That
baseline is more decision-useful than an ungrounded return-on-investment claim.
