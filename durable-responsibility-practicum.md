# Durable-Responsibility Practicum

**Use boundary:** Illustrative 90-minute field exercise; not certification or
proof of production fitness. No practitioner outcome is claimed.
**Scenario:** Transparent fictional composite, Northbridge Exchange.
**Purpose:** Practice turning a completed-looking process into an owned,
testable, and recoverable promise. This does not demonstrate that a workflow,
tool, or learner is effective in production.

## Scenario

Northbridge must resolve a partner's incomplete-shipment dispute within ten
business days. A generated choreography requests carrier evidence, calculates
a candidate credit, and submits it to finance. On day seven, carrier evidence
is missing. Finance accepted a credit request but its acknowledgement was lost.
The status view is green because all workers are healthy. The partner is still
waiting and a retry could repeat a financial effect.

## Run sheet

| Minutes | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 | Name promise, waiting party, deadline, and acceptable endings | Ledger opening row |
| 15–30 | Separate execution, progress, and closure; assign owners | State and authority decisions |
| 30–45 | Decide timeout, duplicate, compensation, and human escalation rules | Failure/approval record |
| 45–65 | Write five adverse tests | Time-and-Failure Test Plan excerpt |
| 65–80 | Decide continue, condition, escalate, compensate, or recover | Responsibility receipt |
| 80–90 | Compare with the annotated answer and retain one unknown | Review note |

## Exercises

1. State what Northbridge owes and what evidence permits closure without carrier evidence.
2. Name who may extend the deadline, authorize a credit, and decide ambiguous acceptance.
3. Define the retry rule after a lost acknowledgement; prohibit an unexamined second credit.
4. Add delay, duplicate, partial-effect, abandoned-human-task, and policy-change tests.
5. Record the owner and due point for every unresolved consequence.

## Annotated answer example

| Decision | One defensible answer | Why it is stronger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promise | “Resolve the partner dispute with an authorized decision, notice, and appeal path by the business deadline.” | It names an outcome, not a request sequence. |
| Day-seven delay | Assign partner operations to decide an extension or exception; the carrier adapter cannot own the partner promise. | A waiting dependency is not an owner. |
| Lost acknowledgement | Mark the credit as `UNKNOWN`; query by operation identity before any retry and route ambiguity to finance authority. | A timeout does not prove absence of effect. |
| Closure | Close only after decision, effect or rejection evidence, partner notice, and retained appeal path are recorded. | Worker health and a terminal state are insufficient. |
| Agent boundary | The agent may assemble evidence and recommend; it may not authorize credit, extend a deadline, or close the dispute. | Useful generation does not inherit workflow authority. |

## Evidence boundary

Freeze fixtures and retain missed ownership, unsafe retries, confusion, and
facilitator intervention before claiming transfer or usability. This scenario
is fictional and its timings, roles, and outcomes are not observations.
