# Durable Workflows Completed-Example Packet

**Use boundary:** Illustrative completed-example packet; not certification or
proof of production fitness

**Packet evidence state:** `constructed` for Northbridge examples; `scenario`
for Aster Vale examples. Every exercise and result remains `planned` / `unrun`.

This packet shows how a reader might complete the five canonical companion
assets. Completion demonstrates template usage only. It does **not** validate a
workflow, a template, an authority model, a failure response, a migration, or a
business outcome. It establishes no usability, technical, business, privacy,
accessibility, or safety result.

## Disclosure and continuity

- **Northbridge Exchange:** an authorized fictional composite. The five
  Northbridge examples form one constructed review branch,
  `NB-DURABLE-01`. They are not incident records, transcripts, measurements,
  or John Briggs's lived experience.
- **Aster Vale Observatory:** an unrelated fictional scenario created to show
  transfer into a scientific observing-campaign domain. It is not a real
  organization, study, operation, or experience report.
- **Practitioner log:** a blank instrument for a future blinded exercise. It is
  marked `planned` / `unrun`; no participant, observation, score, or conclusion
  is represented.

All design values are `constructed`, `scenario`, `proposed`, or `unknown` as
marked. Planned challenges stay `unrun` until inputs, execution, outputs,
limits, and artifact locations are retained.

## How to use the packet

1. Read an example only after reading the corresponding blank asset.
2. Notice how `unknown` remains explicit instead of being filled with invented
   certainty.
3. Use the linked focused worksheet as an applicable blank when a Chapter 5–8
   decision is contested.
4. Copy the *method*, not the fictional values, into a fresh blank.
5. Keep every planned challenge `unrun` until a retained result warrants a
   narrower claim.

Again, a completed-looking record demonstrates form usage, not workflow
validity or practitioner usability.
