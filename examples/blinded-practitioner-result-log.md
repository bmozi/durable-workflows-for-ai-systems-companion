This is an empty result log for a future blinded practitioner exercise. It is
not a claim that a practitioner has used, understood, preferred, or benefited
from any companion asset. It contains no usability, technical, business,
privacy, accessibility, or safety result.

Do not give a participant the completed Northbridge or Aster Vale examples
before the session. Supply an independently prepared fictional scenario that
does not contain expected field names, target classifications, book slogans, or
model answers. The facilitator may identify the blank asset being evaluated,
but must not steer the participant toward preferred vocabulary or decisions.

## Consent and privacy boundary

Before recording a future session:

- obtain informed, voluntary agreement for the stated exercise and recording
  method;
- do not record participant names, employers, clients, real incidents,
  production identifiers, credentials, or sensitive personal/business data;
- use a random participant code and fictional inputs only;
- permit the participant to skip a question or stop;
- do not record audio, video, screen content, or quotations unless separately
  authorized;
- define retention, access, deletion, and correction rules before the session;
  and
- mark any accidental sensitive disclosure for governed removal rather than
  copying it into this log.

These boundaries are proposed. They do not establish legal sufficiency,
privacy, accessibility, research ethics, or participant protection.

## Stop conditions

Stop the exercise and do not treat the session as clean evidence when consent
is absent or withdrawn, real sensitive material enters the fixture, the
facilitator reveals expected answers, required artifacts are lost, asset
versions change mid-session without recording the change, or the participant
cannot proceed because of an unaddressed access need.

This log stays `planned` / `unrun` until an authorized exercise occurs and its
inputs, method, observations, limitations, and artifacts are retained. Even
then, a result would be bounded to the participants, scenarios, asset versions,
facilitation, and conditions actually observed.
