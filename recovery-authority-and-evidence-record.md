# Recovery Authority-and-Evidence Record

**Use boundary:** Illustrative field tool; not certification or proof of
production fitness

**Validation state:** `unrun`. Completing this record does not prove that the
classification is correct, a repair is authorized, an escaped effect was
reversed, retained evidence is complete, or the business promise was resolved.

Use this worksheet for one contested stuck, poisoned, orphaned, incompatible,
or ambiguous workflow case. Record the authorized cross-workflow disposition
in the Responsibility-and-Progress Brief or Compensation-and-Failure Matrix.
When recovery involves unassigned, expired, delegated, substituted, or
conflicting human work, route the same stable decision ID to the Human
Approval-and-Escalation Map. Plan challenges in the Time-and-Failure Test Plan.
Keep observed or tested results in a separately retained evidence package.

## 4. Effect state

Existence, cardinality, and response are independent. One operation identity
may have multiple effects, and one effect may be confirmed, duplicated, and
irreversible simultaneously. An absence is bounded to the named source,
consistency condition, and observation time; it is not proof that no effect
exists.

| Effect identity | Durable operation identity | Authority/source, observation time, and consistency boundary | Existence/evidence: confirmed, bounded-absent, conflicting, or `unknown` | Cardinality/identity: intended only, duplicate, or unknown | Response: reversible, conditionally reversible, compensable, irreversible, residual harm, or unknown | Evidence/confidence | Current owner and next query |
