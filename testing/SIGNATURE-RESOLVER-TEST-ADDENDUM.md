# Durable Responsibility Ledger Test Addendum

**State:** `PREPARED/UNRUN`
**Scope:** Additional test route for the Durable Responsibility Ledger. This
does not alter, replace, or claim execution of the existing companion packet.

## Purpose

Test whether a practitioner can use the Ledger to identify the responsibility
gaps hidden by a plausible workflow description, then resolve each Ledger row
to the correct canonical tool without coaching.

## Minimum controlled task

Give a participant a short, unfamiliar long-running process with a delayed
dependency, an ambiguous external effect, and a pending human decision. Provide
the Ledger and linked canonical blank tools, but not a completed example.

Ask the participant to:

1. record the beneficiary, promise, business owner, technical operating owner,
   deadline, current state, and next authorized transition;
2. choose the required canonical record for effect ambiguity, human approval,
   change, and failure challenge; and
3. state what would count as acceptable closure and what remains unknown.

## Retained observations

Record task time, skipped required fields, duplicate or conflicting current
decisions, unsupported closure claims, unresolved links, participant questions,
and the participant's explanation of the open promise. Keep exact inputs and
anonymized output only with authorized recruitment and any required consent.

## Failure and stop conditions

Treat the tool as needing revision if a participant can complete a neat Ledger
while leaving an ownerless nonterminal state, undefined deadline, unauthorized
transition, or unsupported closure claim. Stop the session if the scenario or
participant data exceeds approved scope. A successful completion does not prove
workflow correctness, usability generally, safety, or business value.
